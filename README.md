## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA 1 is a 2D action role-playing game originally developed by Gamevil and released for mobile platforms in 2009. The restored PC edition was released on August 31, 2026, with development credited to Com2uS Holdings and CFK Co., Ltd. Players guide Regret through a story-driven fantasy adventure featuring real-time combat, exploration, quests, character classes, skill trees, equipment management, and good-and-evil choices.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer provides quality-of-life and progression options for ZENONIA 1. It can modify health, SP, experience, gold, hunger, equipment durability, inventory weight, combat damage, and alignment values while preserving the game's real-time action RPG systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce grinding, experiment with Warrior, Paladin, or Assassin builds, test equipment and skill trees, revisit the story quickly, or customize the difficulty of the single-player campaign.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Keeps Regret's HP from decreasing during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Gold** — Provides unlimited gold for purchasing equipment, supplies, repairs, and other services.
- **No Hunger** — Stops the hunger meter from decreasing while traveling and questing.

- **Infinite SP** — Prevents SP from being consumed when using class skills.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Causes standard enemies to fall after a successful attack.

### 📌 Progression

- **Unlimited Experience** — Allows enemies and quests to grant enhanced or unlimited experience for faster leveling.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **No Durability Loss** — Prevents equipped weapons and armor from losing durability.
- **No Inventory Weight** — Removes or greatly increases inventory weight restrictions.
- **Unlimited Consumables** — Prevents usable food, potions, and other consumable items from decreasing.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

ZENONIA 1 uses direct movement through connected areas rather than turn-based navigation. Explore towns, fields, caves, and dungeons while watching the minimap and managing hunger. The trainer's health, hunger, and inventory options are most useful during long journeys away from merchants and repair services.

### Tools & Inventory

Regret can carry weapons, armor, accessories, talismans, food, potions, quest items, and other loot. Equipment affects combat performance and can lose durability, while carried items contribute to weight limits. Enable No Durability Loss or No Inventory Weight when testing large equipment collections, and use Infinite Gold for purchases, repairs, and item upgrades.

### World & Environment

The campaign combines a main story with optional quests, exploration, shops, equipment upgrades, and choices that influence Regret's alignment toward good or evil. Unlimited Experience can speed up class development, while Alignment Control lets players maintain a preferred route when replaying story decisions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in real time against enemies encountered directly in the world. Regret uses basic attacks, class-specific skills, equipment bonuses, HP, and SP to survive battles. Infinite Health and Infinite SP support skill testing, while One-Hit Defeat is useful for quickly clearing ordinary enemies and revisiting completed areas.
